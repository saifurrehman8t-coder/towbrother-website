When to call for car recovery

  • Engine failure, overheating or gearbox problem
  • Car won't start because of a dead battery or starter fault
  • Accident damage — car not safe to drive
  • Flat tyre with no spare, or wheel damage
  • Car stuck in a ditch, mud, rain water or basement parking
  • Non-running car that needs to go to a workshop, body shop or showroom
  • Car bought or sold that needs to be moved without driving
