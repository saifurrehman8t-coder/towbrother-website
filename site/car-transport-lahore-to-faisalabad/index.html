Lahore to Faisalabad car shifting, door to door

Tow Brothers collects your car from your door in Lahore and delivers it to your address in Faisalabad — or brings it from Faisalabad to Lahore. The car travels on a recovery vehicle, so there is no extra mileage, no wear and no risk of a breakdown on the way.

Route details

  • Distance: about 180–190 km
  • Route: the motorway or Sheikhupura road
  • Timing: Usually delivered within a few hours.
  • Vehicles: cars, SUVs, 4x4s, vans and bikes — running or non-running

How it works

  1. Send details — pickup address in Lahore, drop-off address in Faisalabad, car model.
  2. Get your price — confirmed before we book the trip.
  3. Pickup — we load and strap the car at your door.
  4. Delivery — the car is unloaded and handed over at the destination.

Before pickup

  • Keep a copy of the registration book and your CNIC ready
  • Remove valuables and loose items from the car
  • Note any existing scratches or dents together with our driver

Broken down on the way to Faisalabad?
