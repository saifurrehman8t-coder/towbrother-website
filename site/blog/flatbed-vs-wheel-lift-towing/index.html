Flatbed vs Wheel-Lift Towing: Which Does Your Car Need?

Flatbed or car lifter? Learn which towing method is safe for automatic, 4x4, luxury and manual cars, and how to choose the right recovery vehicle in Lahore.

When you call for car towing in Lahore, you'll usually get one of two vehicles: a flatbed recovery van or a wheel-lift car lifter. Choosing the right one protects your car.

Wheel-lift (car lifter)

A lifter raises either the front or rear wheels and tows the car on its other two wheels.

  • Good for: manual hatchbacks and sedans, short city trips
  • Pros: fast, fits tight streets, usually cheaper
  • Cons: not suitable for most automatics, 4x4s or damaged cars

Flatbed

The whole car is winched onto a flat bed and strapped down. No wheels touch the road.

  • Good for: automatic, CVT, hybrid, electric, 4x4/AWD, luxury, low and accident-damaged cars, long distances
  • Pros: safest option, no wear on the car
  • Cons: bigger vehicle, may cost a little more

Quick rule

If your car is automatic, 4x4, luxury, low or damaged — ask for a flatbed. If it's a manual car going a short distance, a lifter is usually fine. Not sure? Tell us the model and we'll advise. See our flatbed towing and car lifter services.
