Lahore towing service you can count on

Tow Brothers is a local towing service in Lahore. We tow broken-down, damaged and non-running cars from roads, homes, offices, parking plazas and workshops. Whether you need a quick tow from Gulberg to a workshop on Mcleod Road or a long tow from Lahore to Islamabad, we handle it.

Towing methods we use

Flatbed towing

The whole car sits on the bed of the truck. Best for automatic cars, 4x4s, luxury cars, low cars and accident-damaged vehicles. No wheels touch the road.

Wheel-lift towing

Two wheels are lifted and the car is towed on its other two wheels. Quick and cost-effective for most manual hatchbacks and sedans over short city distances.

Not sure which you need? Tell us your car model and the problem — we will send the right truck. Read our guide: flatbed vs wheel-lift towing.

Frequently asked questions

Do you tow automatic cars?

Yes. Automatic cars should be moved on a flatbed so the transmission is not damaged. Tell us your car is automatic and we will send a flatbed.

Can you tow a car with no keys?

Usually yes, with a flatbed or dollies. Tell us when you call so we bring the right equipment.

Do you tow from basement parking?

Most basements can be handled. Share the clearance height or a photo on WhatsApp and we will confirm the right vehicle.
