Common breakdowns we recover in Lahore

  • Overheating — very common in summer traffic
  • Clutch or gearbox failure — stop-start traffic on Ferozepur Road, Multan Road and the Mall wears them out
  • Electrical faults — dead battery, alternator or starter
  • Fuel problems — empty tank, fuel pump failure
  • Timing belt or engine failure
  • Water in engine after driving through flooded roads

Fix on the spot or recover?

If it's a dead battery or a flat tyre, we try to get you going with roadside assistance. If the car can't be driven safely, we recover it to your mechanic or your home.
