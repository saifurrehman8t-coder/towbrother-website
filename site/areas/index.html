Hanjarwal & Multan Road, Lahore

Tow Brothers is based in Hanjarwal, near the Pepsi warehouse, so Hanjarwal and the Multan Road belt is our home ground.

Thokar Niaz Baig, Lahore

Thokar Niaz Baig is where the Lahore–Islamabad Motorway (M-2), Multan Road, Raiwind Road and Canal Road all come together.

DHA Lahore

Defence (DHA) Lahore covers Phase 1 to Phase 9 and beyond, with many automatic, luxury and imported cars on its roads.

Gulberg, Lahore

Gulberg is Lahore's commercial heart, with heavy traffic, office plazas and malls.

Johar Town, Lahore

Johar Town is a large residential and commercial area with busy roads around Emporium Mall, the Expo Centre and Khayaban-e-Firdousi.

Bahria Town Lahore

Bahria Town Lahore is a large gated community off the western end of Canal Road.

Model Town, Lahore

Model Town is one of Lahore's oldest planned neighbourhoods, with blocks A to R around the main circular road and park.

Wapda Town, Lahore

Wapda Town sits between Johar Town and Raiwind Road, a quieter residential area where most recovery calls are dead batteries, workshop shifting and morning breakdowns..

Allama Iqbal Town, Lahore

Allama Iqbal Town is a dense, busy neighbourhood with markets like Moon Market and Karim Block.

Township & Green Town, Lahore

Township and Green Town in south Lahore are busy residential and market areas along Ferozepur Road and Akbar Chowk..

Lahore Cantt & Airport

Lahore Cantonment covers Walton, Sarwar Road, Fortress Stadium, Bedian Road and the Allama Iqbal International Airport area.

Garden Town & Faisal Town, Lahore

Garden Town and Faisal Town are central Lahore neighbourhoods near Kalma Chowk and Barkat Market, with many offices, schools and hospitals..

Valencia, Lake City & Raiwind Road, Lahore

The Raiwind Road belt — Valencia, Lake City, Izmir Town and the newer housing societies — is growing fast.

Canal Road, Lahore

Canal Road runs across Lahore from Thokar Niaz Baig in the west to Harbanspura and Jallo in the east, through a series of underpasses.

Lahore Ring Road

The Lahore Ring Road is a high-speed route around the city, connecting Shahdara, the airport, DHA and Thokar areas.

Shahdara & GT Road, Lahore

Shahdara, across the Ravi bridge, connects Lahore to GT Road and the routes towards Muridke, Kala Shah Kaku and Gujranwala..

Mall Road & Old City, Lahore

Mall Road, Anarkali, Data Darbar and the Walled City area have heavy traffic, narrow lanes and limited parking — a skilled recovery team makes all the difference..

Sabzazar & Mustafa Town, Lahore

Sabzazar, Mustafa Town and Awan Town are right next to our Hanjarwal base, so recovery vans can usually reach this neighbourhood very quickly..

Samanabad, Lahore

Samanabad is a busy, older part of west-central Lahore between Multan Road, Ichhra and Gulshan-e-Ravi, with crowded markets and narrow streets..

Gulshan-e-Ravi, Lahore

Gulshan-e-Ravi sits in west Lahore near Bund Road and the Ravi, a dense residential area with heavy traffic at peak hours..

Shadman, Ichhra & Mozang, Lahore

Shadman, Ichhra and Mozang are central Lahore areas packed with markets, hospitals and offices around Jail Road, Ferozepur Road and Shadman Chowk..

Cavalry Ground & Askari, Lahore

Cavalry Ground and the Askari housing schemes are quiet, well-planned areas in the Cantt belt between Gulberg, DHA and the airport..

Harbanspura & Shalimar, Lahore

Harbanspura, Shalimar, Baghbanpura and Mughalpura make up east Lahore along GT Road and the eastern end of Canal Road..

Bedian Road, Lahore

Bedian Road runs south-east from DHA and Cantt through farmhouses and new housing schemes, where breakdowns can leave you far from a workshop..

Raiwind, Lahore

Raiwind, at the far end of Raiwind Road, has a growing number of housing schemes and heavy traffic during large gatherings..

EME Society & West Canal, Lahore

EME Society and the housing schemes along the western end of Canal Road and Multan Road are close to Thokar Niaz Baig and our Hanjarwal base..
